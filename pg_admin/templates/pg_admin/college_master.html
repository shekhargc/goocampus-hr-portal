College Master

The single source of truth for every Indian PG college — Medical colleges and DNB hospitals as two separate masters, each with its course catalogue. The cut-off / predictor data and (soon) stipend–bond–penalty all hang off this one master, so an old name, a new name or a spelling variant all open the same college profile.
