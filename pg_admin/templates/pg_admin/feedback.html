Doctor Feedback

What doctors say about the goocampus.in dashboard. Asked once — after their 3rd visit day or when they get a seat — plus a Feedback button any time. One feedback per doctor: they can reopen and edit it (changes are kept in History). 1–3 stars raise an alert for a call-back (status "Call back"); once you mark it Resolved, the doctor is offered the Google review on their next visit. Every doctor who gives 4–5 stars is offered the Google review straight away.
