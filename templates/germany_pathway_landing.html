Why Germany

Why Indian Doctors Choose Germany for PG

Germany offers a unique combination of world-class training, financial independence, and a clear path to permanent residency for medical professionals.

🏥

World-Class Healthcare

Train in modern infrastructure with advanced equipment in one of the world's best healthcare systems.

💰

Zero Tuition Fees

No tuition fees for most PG courses in Germany. The cost of medical education is next to zero.

💼

Earn While You Train

PG courses include on-the-job training with a monthly stipend of around €2,700 during training.

🎓

Facharzt Title

After completing training, you receive the title of Facharzt (specialist) — recognized across Europe.

🏠

Permanent Residency

Your chances of getting permanent German residency increase significantly after completing your PG.

📈

High Demand

Germany faces a massive shortage of qualified doctors. Hospitals actively recruit foreign Non-EU doctors.

The Process

Your Journey to Medical PG in Germany

A structured pathway from language training in India to your permanent medical license in Germany.

1
7–8 Months

German Language Training — A1, A2, B1

Start with online German language classes from Level A1 to B1 in India. Appear for A2 level exam at Goethe Institute (GooCampus covers exam fees). This certificate is required for your visa application.

🇮🇳 In India · Online Mode
2
~5 Months

Degree Evaluation — Defizitbescheid

Submit all attested and notarized documents for evaluation by German authorities. GooCampus handles translation through native German translators and lawyers. Focus on language classes during this processing time.

🇮🇳 India → 🇩🇪 Germany (Document Processing)
3
~1 Month

Receive Defizitbescheid & Apply for Visa

Once the Defizitbescheid is received, prepare and submit visa documents to the German Embassy. GooCampus assists with completing the visa file and embassy appointment.

🇮🇳 India · German Embassy
4
3–4 Months

Advanced Language Training — B2 & C1

Continue learning advanced German (B2 to C1) in Germany with a special course designed for doctors. This is critical for the medical language exam that follows.

🇩🇪 In Germany · Special Doctors Course
5
~1 Month

FSP Exam — Fachsprachprüfung

A 1-hour medical language exam with three parts: Patient Case History (20 min), Documentation (20 min), and Doctor-to-Doctor Conversation (20 min). Results declared same day. Unlimited attempts every 3 months.

🇩🇪 In Germany
6
2 Years

Temporary Medical License & Clinical Practice

On passing FSP, receive a 2-year temporary medical license. Start working as an assistant doctor earning around €4,500/month (plus OT/night shift bonuses). Begin your clinical practice in German hospitals.

🇩🇪 In Germany · Hospitals
7
5 Years PG

Approbation Exam → Permanent License → PG

Pass the Approbation/Knowledge exam (3 attempts, 50% to pass) to become a permanently licensed doctor in Germany. Then start your 5-year PG specialization — Cardiology, Urology, Cardiac Surgery, and more.

🇩🇪 In Germany · Universities & Hospitals

Eligibility

Who Can Apply?

If you hold an MBBS degree and are ready to invest in learning German, you can pursue this pathway.

🎓

MBBS Degree

Indian MBBS or equivalent foreign medical degree. Internship completion preferred.

🗣️

German Language

Proficiency at C1-C2 level required. GooCampus trains you from scratch — A1 to C1.

📝

FSP Exam

Pass the Fachsprachprüfung (medical language exam) for a temporary license. Unlimited attempts.

🏆

Approbation Exam

Pass the Knowledge Exam within 2 years for permanent license. 3 attempts, 50% pass mark.
