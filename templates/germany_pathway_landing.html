Why Germany

Why Indian Doctors Choose Germany for PG

Germany offers world-class training, financial independence, and a clear path to permanent residency for medical professionals.

🏥

World-Class Healthcare

Train in modern infrastructure with advanced equipment. Charité, LMU Klinikum, Heidelberg — the best in Europe.

💰

Zero Tuition Fees

No tuition fees for Weiterbildung. This is paid hospital employment, not a tuition-based course.

💼

Earn While You Train

€4,800–€5,500/month gross as Assistenzarzt. Even during temporary license phase, earn ~€2,700/month.

🎓

Facharzt Title

Receive the Facharzt (specialist) title — recognized across the EU, valued by UK GMC, ECFMG, and AHPRA.

🏠

Fast Permanent Residency

Blue Card holders with B2 German can get PR in just 21 months. Germany now allows dual nationality.

📈

Massive Doctor Shortage

20,000+ vacancies yearly. Hospitals actively recruit non-EU doctors. Year-round hiring, especially Feb & Oct.

Your Roadmap to Medical PG in Germany

A structured pathway from language training in India to your permanent medical license and Facharzt specialization in Germany.

Start with online German language classes from A1 to B1 in India. Appear for A2 level exam at Goethe Institute (GooCampus covers exam fees). This certificate is required for your visa application.

Submit all attested and notarized documents for evaluation by German authorities. GooCampus handles translation through native German translators and lawyers. Focus on language classes during this processing time.

Receive Defizitbescheid & Apply for Visa

Once the Defizitbescheid is received, prepare and submit visa documents to the German Embassy. GooCampus assists with completing the visa file and embassy appointment. D16 visa fee is included in the package.

🇮🇳 India · German Embassy

3–4 Months

Advanced Language Training — B2 & C1 (Doctors Course)

Continue advanced medical German (B2 to C1) in Germany with a specialized course for doctors. Covers patient communication, clinical documentation, Arztbrief writing — critical for FSP exam.

🇩🇪 In Germany · Special Doctors Course
5
~1 Month

FSP Exam — Fachsprachprüfung

A 1-hour medical language exam: Patient Case History (20 min), Documentation (20 min), Doctor-to-Doctor Conversation (20 min). Results same day. Unlimited attempts every 3 months. FSP training with native German teacher included.

🇩🇪 In Germany · Ärztekammer
6
2 Years

Temporary Medical License & Clinical Practice

On passing FSP, receive a 2-year Berufserlaubnis (temporary medical license). Start working as an assistant doctor in German hospitals. Begin earning immediately with OT and night shift bonuses on top.

💰 Earning: ~€4,500/month + bonuses
🇩🇪 In Germany · Hospitals
7
5 Years PG

Approbation Exam → Permanent License → Facharzt

Pass the Approbation/Kenntnisprufung exam (3 attempts, 50% pass mark) to become a permanently licensed doctor. Then pursue your 5-year Facharzt specialization — Cardiology, Surgery, Internal Medicine, and more.

💰 Specialist salary: €4,800–€5,500/month gross

Eligibility

Who Can Apply?

If you hold an MBBS degree and are ready to invest in learning German, you can pursue this pathway.

🎓

MBBS Degree

Indian MBBS or equivalent foreign medical degree from NMC-approved, WDOMS-listed institution. Internship completion required.

🗣️

German Language

B2 for document filing, C1 medical German for FSP and hospital work. GooCampus trains you from scratch — A1 to C1.

📝

FSP Exam

Pass the Fachsprachprüfung (medical language exam) for temporary license. Unlimited attempts every 3 months.

🏆

Approbation Exam

Pass the Kenntnisprufung within 2 years for permanent license. 3 attempts, 50% pass mark. Common for Indian MBBS holders.
