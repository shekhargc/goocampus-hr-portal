Germany PGCP — Our Services

GooCampus provides end-to-end support across two phases — everything you need in India before departure, and on-ground support after you arrive in Germany.

🇮🇳 Phase 1

Services in India

Pre-departure preparation — language training, documentation, visa processing, and financial setup before you travel to Germany.

SERVICE 01

German Language Preparation A1 – B1

Structured German language training from beginner (A1) through intermediate (B1) level, specifically designed for medical professionals. Covers everyday German and basic medical vocabulary to prepare you for the B2 medical course in Germany.

LanguageA1–B1Pre-departure
SERVICE 02

Document Preparation for Defizitbescheid

Complete assistance with gathering, organizing, and preparing all documents required for the Defizitbescheid (deficit letter) application — including degree certificates, transcripts, medical council registration, and internship completion letters.

DocumentationDefizitbescheid
SERVICE 03

Letter of Recommendation Assistance

Guidance on obtaining strong Letters of Recommendation from your professors and clinical supervisors, formatted to meet German institutional standards.

DocumentationLOR
SERVICE 04

Statement of Purpose Writing

Professional support in drafting a compelling Statement of Purpose that highlights your medical background, motivation for pursuing PG in Germany, and career goals — tailored for German authorities and institutions.

DocumentationSOP
SERVICE 05

Defizitbescheid / Deficit Letter Application

End-to-end management of your Defizitbescheid application — the official document from German authorities identifying any gaps in your medical qualification that need to be bridged before Approbation. GooCampus handles submission and follow-up.

ApplicationCritical Step
SERVICE 06

Visa Documentation & Appointment Assistance

Complete visa documentation preparation including cover letter, proof of funds, insurance, and appointment booking at the German Embassy/Consulate. We ensure your application package is complete and error-free.

VisaEmbassy
SERVICE 07

Visa Fee & Interview Preparation

Mock interview sessions covering common visa interview questions, guidance on presenting your case confidently, and assistance with visa fee payment procedures.

VisaInterview Prep
SERVICE 08

Foreign Exchange Services

Assistance with foreign exchange requirements — currency conversion guidance, best rates, and compliance with RBI regulations for educational remittances under LRS.

FinancialForex
SERVICE 09

Blocked Account Opening

Help setting up a German Blocked Account (Sperrkonto) — a mandatory requirement for the student/job-seeker visa showing proof of financial sustenance during your stay in Germany.

FinancialSperrkonto
SERVICE 10

Travel & Health Insurance Assistance

Guidance on selecting appropriate travel insurance for your journey and health insurance that meets German visa requirements and covers you during your initial stay.

InsuranceTravel
🇩🇪 Phase 2

Services in Germany

On-ground support after arrival — advanced language training, exam preparation, clinical placements, and settlement assistance throughout your Germany journey.

SERVICE 11

German Language Training B2 & C1 (Doctors Course)

Specialized medical German language training from B2 to C1 level, covering clinical terminology, patient communication, medical documentation, and the specific language skills needed for the FSP exam. Taught by instructors experienced in training international doctors.

LanguageB2–C1 MedicalIn Germany
SERVICE 12

Document Translation & Attestation

Certified translation of all your Indian medical documents into German by sworn translators, plus apostille and attestation services required by German medical authorities.

DocumentationTranslation
SERVICE 13

Document Evaluation / Eingangsbestatigung

Assistance with getting your medical qualifications evaluated and recognized by the relevant German state authority (Landesprufungsamt). The Eingangsbestatigung confirms receipt and begins the formal recognition process.

RecognitionEvaluation
SERVICE 14

Airport Pickup & Accommodation Assistance

Welcome service at the German airport on arrival, followed by assistance finding suitable accommodation — whether shared apartments, student housing, or private rentals near your language school or hospital.

SettlementArrival
SERVICE 15

City Registration & Bank Account Opening

Guidance through the mandatory Anmeldung (city registration) process at the Burgeramt, plus assistance opening a German bank account — both essential first steps for any new resident.

SettlementAnmeldung
SERVICE 16

FSP Training & Exam Booking Assistance

Comprehensive preparation for the Fachsprachprufung (FSP) — the medical German language proficiency exam. Includes mock exams, clinical scenario practice, and assistance with booking your FSP exam date at the Arztekammer.

Exam PrepFSPCritical Step
SERVICE 17

Guidance for Approbation Exams

Support and guidance for the Kenntnisprufung (knowledge exam) or other Approbation pathway exams, including study resources, exam strategy, and booking assistance. Passing these exams is required for the full German medical license.

Exam PrepApprobationCritical Step
SERVICE 18

Observership Placement (2–3 months, Private Clinic)

Placement in a private German clinic for a 2–3 month observership — gain hands-on exposure to the German healthcare system, build clinical vocabulary, and strengthen your profile for residency applications.

ClinicalObservership
SERVICE 19

Temporary Employment Registration

Assistance with registering for temporary employment (Berufserlaubnis) — allowing you to work in a clinical setting under supervision while completing your Approbation requirements. Earn a stipend of approximately €2,700/month.

EmploymentBerufserlaubnis
SERVICE 20

Visa Extension Support

Assistance with extending your German residence permit/visa as your training progresses — including documentation, appointment booking at the Auslanderbehorde, and compliance with immigration requirements.

VisaExtension
SERVICE 21

Job Guidance after Approbation

Career counselling and job placement guidance after obtaining your Approbation (full medical license). Assistance with CV preparation, interview coaching, and navigating the German medical job market to secure a Facharzt (specialist training) position earning €4,500+/month.

CareerFacharzt
SERVICE 22

Health Insurance Assistance

Help selecting and enrolling in the right German health insurance plan — whether public (gesetzliche) or private (private) — based on your employment status and needs. Health insurance is mandatory in Germany.

InsuranceHealth
SERVICE 23

PR (Permanent Residency) Guidance

Long-term settlement support — guidance on applying for Niederlassungserlaubnis (permanent residence permit) after completing your Facharzt training, including documentation and eligibility assessment.

ImmigrationPRLong-term
