Everything You Need in One Place

Our HR portal centralises employee management, leave tracking, and organisational oversight into a single, easy-to-use platform.

Leave Management

Apply for annual, sick, or casual leave with a streamlined approval workflow. Managers can review and approve requests in real time.

Employee Directory

Browse the full employee roster with department filters, role information, and contact details. Search and find anyone instantly.

Monthly Reports

Generate detailed monthly leave reports with salary deduction calculations, exportable to Excel for payroll processing.

Holiday Calendar

View company-wide holidays, team leave patterns, and plan ahead with an interactive calendar showing all leave events at a glance.

Leave Balance Tracking

Real-time visibility into your available leave balance, with carry-forward calculations aligned to the financial year (April–March).

Org Chart

Visualise the organisational structure with an interactive chart showing reporting lines, departments, and team hierarchies.
