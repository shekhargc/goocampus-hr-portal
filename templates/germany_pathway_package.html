Payment Milestones

Three Milestone-Based Installments

Each payment unlocks the next phase of your Germany PG journey. No hidden fees.

Installment 1
€5,000
Paid at enrolment
End-to-End Document Handling
  • German language preparation A1 – B1 (online, India)
  • Document preparation for Defizitbescheid
  • Document evaluation & German translation
  • Letter of Recommendation assistance
  • Motivation letter writing
  • Contract agreement execution
Installment 2
€5,000
Paid after Installment 1 completion
Defizitbescheid + Visa Phase
  • Defizitbescheid / Deficit letter application
  • Document attestation
  • Visa documentation & appointment
  • Visa fee & interview preparation
  • Foreign exchange services
  • Blocked account opening
  • Travel & health insurance assistance
Installment 3
€5,000
Paid after visa is received
Germany Arrival + FSP + Residency
  • B2 & C1 doctors course (Germany)
  • Airport pickup & accommodation assistance
  • City registration & bank account
  • Document evaluation / Eingangsbestätigung
  • FSP training & exam booking
  • Approbation/Kenntnisprufung exam guidance
  • Temporary employment registration
  • Job guidance after Approbation
  • Health insurance assistance
  • PR (Permanent Residency) guidance

Important Notes

  • D16 visa fee IS included in the GooCampus package
  • Ticket fare and accommodation in Germany are NOT included — candidates use their blocked account (~€1,000 – €1,500/month) for living expenses
  • Blocked account should hold approximately €13,000 – €14,000 (required by German authorities)
  • Employment visa process begins after 1 year of stay — cost NOT covered by GooCampus
  • Observership placement (2–3 months, private clinic) is included in the services
  • Visa extension support is included throughout your stay
