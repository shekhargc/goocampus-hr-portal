💡 Important Note on Blocked Account

The blocked account (~€13,000–€14,000) is required by German authorities as proof of financial support. This money is yours — you withdraw ~€1,000–€1,500/month for living expenses. It is NOT a fee; it's your own savings for your stay in Germany.
