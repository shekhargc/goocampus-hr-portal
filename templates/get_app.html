Use GooCampus as an app on your phone

Add the portal to your home screen and open it like a normal app — no download from any store needed.

On a computer? Scan this with your phone camera

It opens this same page on your phone, where you can add the app to your home screen.

1

Open in Chrome and log in first

Go to goocampus.org in the Chrome browser and log in to your account. The install option only appears once you're signed in.

2

Tap the menu

Tap the three dots at the top-right of Chrome.

3

Tap “Install app”

Depending on your phone it may say “Install app”, “Add to Home screen” or “Install / create shortcut” — tap it. Chrome adds a GooCampus icon to your home screen; open it any time like a normal app.

Important: on iPhone this only works in the Safari browser (not Chrome). If you opened this in another app, tap ••• and choose “Open in Safari” first.
1

Open the portal in Safari

Go to goocampus.org in Safari and open your login page.

2

Tap the Share button

It's the square with an up-arrow, at the bottom of Safari (or top on iPad).

3

Tap “Add to Home Screen”

Scroll down the share menu and tap it.

4

Tap “Add”

The GooCampus icon appears on your home screen. Open it any time like a normal app.
