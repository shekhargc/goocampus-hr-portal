How to set prices
  1. Download the Unique Services Excel (one row per service + provider; prices already set are pre-filled) — or by service for one price per service whatever the provider.
  2. Fill the “Your price (₹)” column with what GooCampus pays for that service. Leave blank to skip a row.
  3. Upload it below, check the preview, then Apply.

A price for a specific provider beats the service's general price. Re-uploading updates prices (history is kept).
