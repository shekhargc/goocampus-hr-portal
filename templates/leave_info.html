Leave Allocation Structure

Every employee at GooCampus is entitled to 25 paid leave days per financial year (April to March). This base allocation is distributed equally across 12 months, giving each employee approximately 2.08 leaves per month.

Leave allocation is cumulative — unused monthly allocation rolls into the next month's balance within the same financial year. For example, if you don't take any leave in April, your May balance will include April's unused allocation.

Three Types of Leave:

Annual Leave — Planned leave for vacations, personal commitments, or any pre-planned absence. Should be applied at least 2 days in advance when possible.

Sick Leave — For illness or medical appointments. Medical documentation may be requested for sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive days.

Casual Leave — For unforeseen or urgent situations that require immediate time off. Limited to short durations.

Half-Day Leave:

Employees can apply for half-day leave (First Half or Second Half) for all three leave types. A half-day leave counts as 0.5 days against your balance.

Late Leave Application:

If you forget to apply for leave before or on the date, you can submit a Late Leave Application with a reason for the delay. Late applications go through the same approval process.

How Leave Deduction Works

When you apply for leave, the system checks whether your available balance covers the request. Your balance is calculated as:

Balance Formula

Available Balance = (Base Allocation: 25) + (Carry Forward) − (Total Days Taken This FY)

Leave deduction is straightforward:

• Full Day leave — deducts 1.0 day from your balance
• Half Day leave (First Half or Second Half) — deducts 0.5 days from your balance

Your balance runs on a monthly allocation cycle. Each month adds 2.08 days to your running balance. If you take more leaves than your accumulated allocation in a given month, the extra is deducted from future months' allocation. The system tracks a running balance so you can always see where you stand.

Negative Balance Warning

If your total leave taken exceeds your total allocation (base + carry forward), your balance will go negative. Negative balances may result in salary deductions as per company policy. Always check your available balance before applying.

Carry Forward & Encashment Policy

At the end of each financial year (March 31), unused leave is handled as follows:

Carry Forward Rules

• Employees with more than 2 unused leave days at year-end are eligible for carry forward.
• A maximum of 8 days can be carried forward to the next financial year.
• Carried forward days are added to your next year's base allocation of 25, giving you up to 33 total days maximum.
• If you have 2 or fewer unused days, no carry forward applies — these days simply lapse.

Leave Encashment Rules

• Employees with more than 8 unused leave days at year-end have an option to encash the excess.
• For example: if you have 12 unused days, you can carry forward up to 8 and encash the remaining 4 days.
• Encashment is optional — you may choose to carry forward only (up to 8 days) and let the rest lapse.
• Encashment is paid at your basic daily salary rate and is processed in the April payroll of the new FY.

Example: End of FY {{ fy_year - 1 }}–{{ fy_year }}

Case 1: Employee A had 1.5 unused days → No carry forward (below 2-day threshold). Balance starts at 25 days for FY {{ fy_year }}–{{ fy_year + 1 }}.
Case 2: Employee B had 5 unused days → All 5 days carry forward. Total allocation for FY {{ fy_year }}–{{ fy_year + 1 }} = 25 + 5 = 30 days.
Case 3: Employee C had 14 unused days → Carries forward 8 days (maximum). Can encash up to 6 days. Total allocation = 25 + 8 = 33 days. Encashment of 6 days paid in April salary.

Leave Approval Process
  1. 1
    Employee Applies for Leave
    Submit your leave application through the CRM portal by selecting the date(s), leave type (annual, sick, or casual), and providing a reason. You can also apply for half-day leave if needed.
  2. 2
    Reporting Manager Reviews
    Your direct reporting manager receives a notification and reviews your leave request. They can see your current balance, team availability, and any overlapping leave in the team before making a decision.
  3. 3
    Approved or Rejected
    The manager approves or rejects the request. If approved, the leave is deducted from your balance immediately. If rejected, your balance remains unchanged and you'll see the rejection reason in your applications.
  4. 4
    Retrieve or Modify (Optional)
    Plans changed? You can retrieve an approved leave (cancels it and restores your balance) or modify it (change date or type). Both actions require providing a reason and go through the approval flow again.
Important Notes

• Pending requests are not deducted from your balance until approved.
• Leave applications should ideally be submitted before the leave date. For emergencies, use the Late Leave Application feature.
• Managers can view team leave calendars to ensure adequate staffing before approving.
• Management (GC001, GC002, GC003) leave applications follow the same process but may have additional approval from senior leadership.
