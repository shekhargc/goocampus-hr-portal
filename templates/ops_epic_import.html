How it works

The relationship CSV links each EPIC record to a UK Registration record via Zoho IDs. The UK Registration CSV maps those Zoho IDs to registration numbers. Records are matched to existing PLAB clients by registration number. Duplicates are detected by registration number + EPIC ID Number.
