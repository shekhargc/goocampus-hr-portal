About the Institution

A Legacy Institution.
A Global Campus.

JSS AHER Mauritius is a degree-awarding autonomous university registered with the Higher Education Commission, Mauritius — backed by JSS Mahavidyapeetha, one of India's most respected educational trusts with 360+ institutions.

🏛️

Degree-Awarding Authority

JSS AHER Mauritius is an autonomous university with full degree-granting authority, recognised by the Higher Education Commission and the Mauritius Qualifications Authority. Your MBBS degree carries global standing.

🎓

CBME-Based Curriculum

Follows the Competency Based Medical Education framework as prescribed by the NMC of India — the same standard followed at JSS Medical College, Mysuru.

🌍

Globally Recognised

Accredited by relevant higher education and quality assurance bodies in Mauritius. Degree recognised for internship pathways in India, UK, US, and other countries.

🏥

Clinical-First Approach

Students begin clinical postings from Semester 3 itself — ensuring maximum bedside exposure throughout the programme.

📚

FMGE & Licensing Exam Coaching

Dedicated preparation for FMGE (India), USMLE, and PLAB built into the curriculum — preparing you for licensing exams worldwide.

🏠 Accommodation Included

On-campus hostel in a gated, secure environment. Double-sharing rooms, male and female hostels fully separate. Vegetarian meals served in the attached canteen. Expanding to 300 rooms.

📦 What's Included

Tuition, lab access, e-library, practical materials, examination fees, and hostel are all covered. Students may need to purchase some textbooks — no other major additional expenses expected.

Refund Policy

50% tuition refund available if withdrawal is within the first 30 working days of programme commencement. Caution deposit is fully refundable at end of programme. Food charges refundable pro-rata with one-month notice.

Admission Requirements

Who Can Apply?

Eligibility criteria vary by country of origin. NEET qualification is mandatory for Indian candidates wishing to practice or pursue PG in India.

🇮🇳

Indian Candidates

Must be NEET-UG qualified (current or previous year score valid)
NEET qualification enables return to India for PG or licence registration
Students planning PG abroad (UK/Australia) may apply — NEET still advisable to keep future options open
Physics, Chemistry, Biology at 10+2 level with 50% or above in these subjects (as per standard NEET eligibility)
🌍

Overseas Candidates (Non-India)

Must satisfy home-country entry requirements for undergraduate medical education
Mauritius candidates: Pass in 3 A-level subjects at HSC with minimum 21 points
English proficiency proof required if English is not the medium of prior education
South African and other African candidates: country-specific entry criteria apply
