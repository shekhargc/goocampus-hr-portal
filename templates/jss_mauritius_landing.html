The MBBS at JSS Academy of Higher Education & Research, Mauritius is a 5-year, English-medium undergraduate medical degree aligned with the National Medical Commission (NMC) of India's Competency-Based Medical Education (CBME) framework. JSS Mauritius is a degree-awarding autonomous university recognised by the Higher Education Commission of Mauritius, and is the Mauritius arm of JSS Mahavidyapeetha — a 360+ institution legacy from India. The programme offers clinical exposure from Semester 3, 1,200+ bed clinical training, and 6,355 teaching hours. Applications are open for the 2026 batch.

Admission Requirements

Who Can Apply?

Eligibility criteria vary by country of origin. NEET qualification is mandatory for Indian candidates wishing to practice or pursue PG in India.

🇮🇳

Indian Candidates

Must be NEET-UG qualified (current or previous year score valid)
NEET qualification enables return to India for PG or licence registration
Students planning PG abroad (UK / Australia) may apply — NEET still advisable to keep future options open
Physics, Chemistry, Biology at 10+2 level with 50% or above in these subjects (standard NEET eligibility)
🌍

Overseas Candidates (Non-India)

Must satisfy home-country entry requirements for undergraduate medical education
Mauritius candidates: Pass in 3 A-level subjects at HSC with minimum 21 points
English proficiency proof required if English is not the medium of prior education
South African and other African candidates: country-specific entry criteria apply

Frequently Asked

Questions, Answered.

The most common questions students and parents ask about MBBS at JSS Mauritius.

Is the JSS Mauritius MBBS degree recognised in India?
Yes. The degree is awarded by an autonomous university registered with the Higher Education Commission, Mauritius. NEET-qualified Indian students can return to India and appear for the FMGE/NExT to register and practice.
When does clinical exposure begin?
From Semester 3 itself. Students begin structured clinical postings at the Government Teaching Hospital and the five regional specialty hospitals — covering all major departments.
Is NEET mandatory?
NEET is mandatory for Indian candidates who wish to practice or pursue PG in India. Students planning to pursue PG abroad can still apply, but having a NEET qualification keeps your future options open.
What is the total cost of the programme?
The total annual cost is approximately USD 14,950 — covering tuition, registration, exam fees, accommodation and food. A one-time refundable caution deposit of USD 1,000 is paid at the start.
Is accommodation included?
Yes. On-campus hostel accommodation is included in the annual fee. Double-sharing rooms, fully separate male and female hostels, in a gated and secure community adjacent to the college.
What about the language of instruction?
All teaching is in English. French and Kreol Morisien are introduced in the Communication Skills module for local clinical interaction in Phase I.
