Top Medical Universities

MBBS in Uzbekistan is a 6-year, English-medium undergraduate medical degree offered at universities recognised by the National Medical Commission (NMC) of India, WHO and WDOMS. Frequently Asked

Questions, Answered.

The most common questions students and parents ask about MBBS in Uzbekistan.

Is MBBS from Uzbekistan valid in India?
Yes, MBBS from Uzbekistan is valid in India — with one condition. Graduates must clear the NExT exam (National Exit Test, which has replaced FMGE) after returning to India. Once cleared, you are fully licensed to practice medicine in India.
Do I need IELTS or TOEFL to study MBBS in Uzbekistan?
No — IELTS and TOEFL are not required for MBBS in Uzbekistan. Universities in Uzbekistan do not ask for English proficiency test scores. Since your Class 12 was completed in English medium, that is considered sufficient proof of language ability.
What is the total cost of MBBS in Uzbekistan?
The total 6-year cost including tuition, hostel, food, and living expenses starts from approximately ₹20L–25L. Tuition is around $3,500/year, and hostel costs $1,200–$1,500 per year. Realistically, plan for ₹25–35L all-in once you add visa renewals and flights.
Is NEET mandatory for MBBS in Uzbekistan?
Yes. NEET is mandatory for all Indian students wishing to pursue MBBS abroad as per NMC regulations. It is also required to appear for the FMGE/NExT after completing MBBS — which is what makes your degree valid to practice in India. General category students need a minimum 50th percentile score; SC/ST/OBC need 40th percentile.
How long does the admission process take?
The admission process is very fast. After submitting your documents, you typically receive your offer letter within 7–10 working days. Visa processing takes an additional 2–3 weeks. Our team handles all paperwork end-to-end.
Is Uzbekistan safe for Indian students?
Yes, Uzbekistan is considered safe for international students. There is a large and growing Indian student community, university campuses have 24/7 security, and Indian embassies are active in supporting students. Indian food options and Indian faculty are available at most universities.
