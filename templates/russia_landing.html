MBBS in Russia is a 6-year, English-medium undergraduate medical programme offered at 55+ government medical universities approved by the National Medical Commission (NMC) of India. The programme covers 5 years of academic study plus 1 year of clinical internship, with hands-on hospital training from Year 3. Total 6-year cost (tuition, hostel, food, visa and living) typically ranges from ₹20L to ₹32L — with no donation or capitation fees. Russian medical degrees are recognised by NMC, WHO, FAIMER, ECFMG and WFME, and graduates can return to India via the FMGE/NExT licensing exam, or pursue PLAB (UK), USMLE (US) or AMC (Australia). Admission Requirements

Who Can Apply?

NEET is mandatory for all Indian students per NMC regulations. Documents are reviewed for admission — no separate university entrance exam is required.

🇮🇳

NEET & Academic Eligibility

NEET-UG qualified — minimum 50th percentile for General (40th for SC/ST/OBC)
Physics, Chemistry & Biology in Class 12 with 50% or above (45% for SC/ST/OBC)
Minimum age: 17 years as on 31 December of the admission year
No university entrance exam — admission based on documents and NEET qualification
Even a NEET score in the 150–200 band may be sufficient — NMC percentile is the qualifying bar

Frequently Asked

Questions, Answered.

The most common questions students and parents ask about MBBS in Russia.

Is the MBBS degree from Russian universities recognised in India?
Yes. Russia has 55+ medical universities approved by the National Medical Commission (NMC) of India. After completing the 6-year programme and clearing the GOZZ graduation exam, Indian students return and appear for FMGE / NExT — and on clearing it, register with the NMC and practise medicine in India.
When does clinical exposure begin?
Hands-on hospital training begins from Year 3 — across departments such as internal medicine, surgery, paediatrics, gynaecology, and emergency medicine, in government-affiliated teaching hospitals.
Is NEET mandatory?
Yes. As per NMC regulations, NEET-UG qualification is mandatory for all Indian students pursuing MBBS abroad — minimum 50th percentile for General (40th for SC/ST/OBC). Even a NEET score in the 150–200 band may be sufficient, since percentile is the qualifying bar.
What is the total cost of the programme?
The estimated total 6-year cost typically ranges from ₹20L to ₹32L — all-inclusive of tuition, hostel, food, visa, travel and personal expenses. Annual tuition at lower-fee universities is approximately ₹2.3L–₹2.8L; higher-fee universities are approximately ₹3L–₹3.5L per year.
Is FMGE/NExT coaching provided?
Yes. FMGE/NExT coaching is provided from 1st or 2nd year itself — not after graduation. Students who begin preparation early significantly improve their India-return outcomes. Top-ranked universities commonly see FMGE/NExT pass rates in the 40–50% band when paired with strong student preparation from Year 4 onward.
What is the language of instruction?
All teaching is in English. Russian language is included as a subject in Year 1 and Year 2 to support clinical interaction with local patients during hospital postings from Year 3 onward.
What is the GOZZ exam?
GOZZ (State Final Attestation) is the mandatory graduation exam at the end of Year 6. It is a three-part assessment — a written MCQ paper, a practical examination on real patients, and an oral viva — that students must clear to receive their Russian MBBS-equivalent degree. After GOZZ, students apostille their records and proceed to FMGE/NExT in India.
