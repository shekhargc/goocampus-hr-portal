Why Vietnam

Southeast Asia's Rising
MBBS Destination.

Vietnam combines affordable medical education, English-medium instruction, genuine clinical exposure, and strong international recognition — making it a compelling option for Indian medical aspirants.

🌍

WHO, IMED & ECFMG Recognised

Medical degrees from Vietnamese universities are recognised by WHO, IMED, and ECFMG — enabling graduates to pursue licensing pathways in India and internationally.

💰

No Donation or Capitation Fees

Admission to Vietnamese medical universities is entirely merit-based. There are no management quota seats or hidden donation charges — a transparent process from start to finish.

🇬🇧

Fully English-Medium Programme

Medical programmes are delivered entirely in English, removing language barriers for Indian students and allowing full focus on academics and clinical training.

✈️

Close to India — 4.5 Hours Away

Vietnam is one of the closest MBBS abroad destinations to India. Flights from major Indian cities to Hanoi or Ho Chi Minh City typically cost between ₹8,000–₹10,000 one way.

🏥

Strong Practical Training

Vietnamese medical universities emphasise hands-on clinical learning at affiliated teaching hospitals with high patient volumes — giving students real-world exposure from the clinical years onward.

💵

Affordable Living Costs

Monthly living expenses in Vietnamese cities are among the lowest of any MBBS abroad destination, keeping the total 6-year cost well within a manageable range for most Indian families.

Admission Requirements

Who Can Apply?

Admission to Vietnamese medical universities is merit-based. NEET qualification is mandatory for Indian students.

📋

Academic & NEET Eligibility

NEET-UG qualified — mandatory for Indian students as per NMC regulations
Physics, Chemistry & Biology in Class 12 with 50% or above in these subjects
Minimum age: 17 years as on 31 December of the admission year
No separate university entrance exam — admission based on documents and NEET qualification
📁

Documents Required

Class 10 & 12 mark sheets and passing certificates
NEET 2026 scorecard
Valid Indian passport (minimum 18 months validity recommended)
Passport-size photographs
Birth certificate
Medical fitness certificate

After MBBS

Your Degree. Multiple Pathways.

A WHO and IMED-recognised MBBS from Vietnam opens pathways to practice and postgraduate training in India, UK, USA, Australia, and other countries through their respective licensing processes.

Practice in India

Clear FMGE/NExT, complete Indian medical registration, begin practice or appear for NEET PG for MD/MS specialisation

Practice in UK

PLAB 1 & 2 pathway — one of the most accessible international routes for Indian medical graduates

USA Residency

USMLE Step 1, 2 & 3 pathway — for US residency programmes and American medical practice

Other Countries

Country-specific licensing exams applicable for Australia (AMC), Canada, Germany, UAE and other destinations

Your Step-by-Step India Return Route

1

Complete 6-Year MBBS in Vietnam including internship year

2

Receive degree certificate and apostille all academic records for India

3

Appear for FMGE/NExT — the Indian licensing exam for foreign medical graduates

4

Complete Indian medical registration with the NMC upon clearing FMGE/NExT

5

Begin licensed practice in India or appear for NEET PG for MD/MS specialisation
