Dong A University · Da Nang

MBBS in Vietnam is a 6-year, English-medium undergraduate medical degree offered at WHO, IMED and ECFMG-recognised universities such as Dong A University (Da Nang), Võ Trường Toản University (Can Tho), Duy Tan University (Da Nang) and Hong Bang International University (Ho Chi Minh City). Annual tuition ranges from USD 4,500 to USD 8,000, with a total 6-year cost of approximately ₹25–30 lakhs including accommodation, food, visa, travel and living expenses. There are no donation or capitation fees. Admission is merit-based: NEET-UG qualification is mandatory for Indian students as per National Medical Commission (NMC) regulations. Graduates are eligible for the FMGE/NExT screening exam to register and practice in India. Applications are open for the 2026 batch.

Frequently Asked

Questions, Answered.

The most common questions students and parents ask about MBBS in Vietnam.

Is the MBBS degree from Vietnam recognised in India?
Yes. Medical degrees from our partner Vietnamese universities are recognised by WHO and IMED. NEET-qualified Indian graduates are eligible to appear for the FMGE/NExT exam, register with the National Medical Commission (NMC), and practice medicine in India.
How long is the MBBS programme in Vietnam?
The MBBS programme in Vietnam is a 6-year, full-time, on-campus degree programme delivered entirely in English, including the internship year.
Is NEET mandatory for MBBS in Vietnam?
Yes. NEET-UG qualification is mandatory for Indian students as per NMC regulations. There is no separate university entrance exam — admission is based on documents and NEET qualification.
What is the total cost of MBBS in Vietnam?
The estimated total 6-year cost — including tuition, accommodation, food, visa, travel, and personal expenses — is approximately ₹25–30 lakhs. Annual tuition at partner universities ranges from USD 4,500 (Dong A) to USD 8,000 (Duy Tan / Hong Bang International).
Are there any donation or capitation fees?
No. Admission to Vietnamese medical universities is entirely merit-based. There are no management quota seats, donations, or capitation fees — the total cost is fully predictable from the start.
What about the language of instruction?
All teaching is in English across the full 6-year programme — removing language barriers for Indian students and allowing full focus on academics and clinical training.
