What is MBBS in Georgia for Indian students in 2026?

MBBS in Georgia is a 6-year English-medium MD (Doctor of Medicine) program offered by NMC-approved universities. Annual tuition fees typically range between $4,800 and $6,500, depending on the university. The estimated total cost for the complete 6-year program — including tuition, accommodation, food, and insurance — ranges from ₹45 lakh to ₹65 lakh. Admission does not require IELTS or any local entrance examination; however, a valid NEET qualifying score is mandatory as per NMC regulations for Indian students. Georgia is currently considered one of the strongest MBBS abroad destinations in terms of country-level FMGE performance trends for India-return graduates in 2026. All universities in Georgia offer 2 intakes — September and March. Choosing the right university is crucial, as FMGE results and academic standards can vary significantly across institutions within Georgia.

Year-by-Year Curriculum

How the 6-year MBBS program is structured in Georgia

1Pre-Clinical

Basic science foundations

Anatomy, histology, embryology, biochemistry, cell biology, and medical terminology. Full structural and chemical groundwork before any clinical contact.

2Pre-Clinical

Body systems and early orientation

Physiology, microbiology, immunology, genetics, and pathology foundations. Some universities begin clinical orientation modules in year 2.

3Para-Clinical

Disease mechanisms and diagnostics

Pathology, pathophysiology, pharmacology, diagnostic methods, and clinical skills introduction. The bridge between classroom science and hospital practice.

4Clinical

First supervised hospital rotations

Internal medicine, surgery, neurology, dermatology, psychiatry, and ENT. Students begin supervised ward exposure in real hospital departments.

5Clinical

Specialties and community medicine

Paediatrics, OBG, emergency medicine, orthopaedics, radiology, oncology, and community health. NExT preparation should begin running in parallel this year.

6Rotations

Advanced clerkships and graduation

Full clinical rotations across departments, elective placements, practical assessments, and graduation requirements. Disciplined NExT preparation is non-negotiable by year 6.
