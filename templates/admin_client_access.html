How a client gets access

A client sees the dashboard only when all of these are true:

  1. They have a login — created automatically when a client registers through the portal. Old/imported clients don’t have one yet (shown as “No login yet”).
  2. Their pathway is switched ON below (Pathway rollout).
  3. Their status is In Process or On Hold. Any other status (Completed, Dropped, Switched…) loses access automatically.
  4. You haven’t manually disabled them (the Admin column).

Pathway rollout

Master switch per pathway: turn the dashboard on/off for a whole pathway at once (only affects clients who have a login). Saving takes effect immediately — clients of an OFF pathway see a “coming soon” screen instead of the dashboard.
